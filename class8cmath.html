  1. 1. find the area of the circle, if diameter = 14 cm (1)
    Radius r = 14/2 = 7 cm.
    Area = πr² = π × 7² = 49π cm² ≈ 153.94 cm³ (exact: 49π cm²).
  2. 2. a cubical tank is 12m long. What is the volume of the tank. (1)
    Cube side = 12 m. Volume = side³ = 12³ = 1,728 m³.

  4. 4. The perimeter of the square feild is 120 m. find the length and the area of the square field. (1)
    Perimeter = 120 m. Side = 120 ÷ 4 = 30 m.
    Area = side² = 30² = 900 m².
  5. 5. Convert 10011₂ into denary numbers. (1)
    1·2⁴ + 0·2³ + 0·2² + 1·2¹ + 1·2⁰ = 16 + 0 + 0 + 2 + 1 = 19.
  6. 6. Convert 1234 into binary numbers. (1)
    Decimal 1234 = binary 10011010010₂.
  7. 7. Write 0.00023 into scientific notation. (1)
    0.00023 = 2.3 × 10⁻⁴.
  8. 8. Find the ration of 500 m and 2 km. (1)
    Convert 2 km = 2000 m. Ratio 500 : 2000 = 1 : 4.
  9. 9. Bread and biscuit are distributed in the ratio of 5:3 for tiffin in a school. If 45 biscuits were served, find the number of breads distributed. (1)
    Biscuits correspond to 3 parts. One part = 45 ÷ 3 = 15.
    Breads = 5 parts = 5 × 15 = 75 breads.
  10. 10. Simplify and express in scientific notation.
    7.89 × 10⁴ − 6.43 × 10³ (1)
    7.89×10⁴ = 78,900. 6.43×10³ = 6,430.
    Difference = 78,900 − 6,430 = 72,470 = 7.247 × 10⁴.
