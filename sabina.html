गणितका प्रश्नहरुको हल (Math Questions Answers)

Question 1:

Percentages and Venn-Diagrams

In an examination, 85% of the students passed in mathematics, 87% in English and 81% in both. If 18 students failed in the examination, find the total number of students and the percentage of students passed in at least one subject. Also, represent the information in a Venn-diagram.

Solution:

हामीलाई दिइएको छ:

Let M be the set of students who passed in Mathematics. (नमादिसस्नफिर)

Let E be the set of students who passed in English.

  • Percentage passed in Mathematics, P(M) = 85%
  • Percentage passed in English, P(E) = 87%
  • Percentage passed in both subjects, P(M ∩ E) = 81%

i. What percent of the students passed in at least one subject? (कम्तीमा एउटा विषयमा पास हुने विद्यार्थीको प्रतिशत कति हो?)

P(M ∪ E) = P(M) + P(E) - P(M ∩ E)

P(M ∪ E) = 85% + 87% - 81% = 91%

Answer: 91% of the students passed in at least one subject.

ii. If 18 students failed in the examination, find the total number of students. (यदि १८ जना विद्यार्थी फेल भए, जम्मा विद्यार्थी संख्या कति होला?)

फेल हुने विद्यार्थीको प्रतिशत = 100% - P(M ∪ E) = 100% - 91% = 9%

प्रश्न अनुसार, ९% विद्यार्थी भनेको १८ जना हो।

9% of Total Students (T) = 18

0.09 * T = 18

T = 18 / 0.09 = 200

Answer: The total number of students is 200.

iii. Represent the above information in a Venn-diagram. (माथिको जानकारीलाई भेन-डाइग्राममा देखाउनुहोस्।)

भेन्डाइग्रामको लागि, हामीले मात्र एउटा विषयमा पास हुने र फेल हुने विद्यार्थीको प्रतिशत पत्ता लगाउनुपर्छ।

  • Only Mathematics: 85% - 81% = 4%
  • Only English: 87% - 81% = 6%
  • Both: 81%
  • Failed: 9%

यसलाई भेन-डाइग्राममा देखाउँदा, दुईवटा गोलाहरू (M र E) एकआपसमा काटिएका हुन्छन्। बीचको भागमा ८१%, M को मात्र भागमा ४% र E को मात्र भागमा ६% हुन्छ। दुबै गोला बाहिरको भागमा ९% हुन्छ।

Question 2:

Income Tax Calculation (आयकरको गणना)

Mr. Shrestha married sales manager in a motorbike company having a monthly salary Rs 75000, a dearness allowance of Rs 2500 and Rs 40000 as a festival allowance. But he pays Rs 48000 as a premium of life insurance. It is given that 1% of social security tax is levied on the income up to Rs 6,00,000, 10% and 20% income tax on the incomes exceeding Rs 6,00,000 to Rs 8,00,000 and on the income exceeding 8,00,000 to Rs 11,00,000.

Solution:

i. Find his annual income. (उहाँको वार्षिक आम्दानी कति हो?)

मासिक तलब = Rs 75,000

मासिक भत्ता = Rs 2,500

वार्षिक आम्दानी = (Monthly Salary + Dearness Allowance) * 12 + Festival Allowance

Annual Income = (Rs 75,000 + Rs 2,500) * 12 + Rs 40,000

Annual Income = Rs 77,500 * 12 + Rs 40,000 = Rs 9,30,000 + Rs 40,000 = Rs 9,70,000

Answer: Mr. Shrestha's annual income is Rs 9,70,000.

ii. What is the taxable income? (कर लाग्ने आम्दानी कति हो?)

कर लाग्ने आम्दानी = Annual Income - Life Insurance Premium

Taxable Income = Rs 9,70,000 - Rs 48,000 = Rs 9,22,000

Answer: The taxable income is Rs 9,22,000.

iii. Calculate the income tax paid by Mr. Shrestha. (उहाँले तिर्नुपर्ने आयकर गणना गर्नुहोस्।)

कर लाग्ने आम्दानी Rs 9,22,000 छ। अब हामी दिइएको स्ल्याब अनुसार कर गणना गर्छौं:

  • Rs 6,00,000 सम्ममा: 1% सामाजिक सुरक्षा कर
    कर = 1% of Rs 6,00,000 = Rs 6,000
  • Rs 6,00,001 देखि Rs 8,00,000 सम्ममा: 10% आयकर
    यस स्ल्याबमा पर्ने रकम = Rs 8,00,000 - Rs 6,00,000 = Rs 2,00,000
    कर = 10% of Rs 2,00,000 = Rs 20,000
  • Rs 8,00,001 देखि Rs 11,00,000 सम्ममा: 20% आयकर
    यस स्ल्याबमा पर्ने रकम = Rs 9,22,000 - Rs 8,00,000 = Rs 1,22,000
    कर = 20% of Rs 1,22,000 = Rs 24,400

जम्मा आयकर = Rs 6,000 + Rs 20,000 + Rs 24,400 = Rs 50,400

Answer: The total income tax paid by Mr. Shrestha is Rs 50,400.

Question 3:

VAT and Discount Calculation (भ्याट र छुटको गणना)

A tourist paid Rs 14125 for a carved wooden window with a discount of 20% and 13% VAT.

Solution:

हामीलाई थाहा छ:

  • भ्याटसहितको मूल्य (Selling Price with VAT) = Rs 14,125
  • छुट दर (Discount Rate) = 20%
  • भ्याट दर (VAT Rate) = 13%

पहिले भ्याट अघिको Selling Price निकालौं:

SP with VAT = SP * (1 + VAT Rate)

14125 = SP * (1 + 0.13)

14125 = SP * 1.13

SP = 14125 / 1.13 = 12500

छुट पछिको मूल्य (Selling Price) Rs 12,500 हो।

i. Find the marked price of the window. (झ्यालको अंकित मूल्य पत्ता लगाउनुहोस्।)

अंकित मूल्य (Marked Price, MP) बाट छुट दिएपछि Selling Price आउँछ।

SP = MP * (1 - Discount Rate)

12500 = MP * (1 - 0.20)

12500 = MP * 0.80

MP = 12500 / 0.80 = 15625

Answer: The marked price of the window is Rs 15,625.

ii. Obtain the discount offered. (दिइएको छुट रकम पत्ता लगाउनुहोस्।)

छुट रकम = Marked Price - Selling Price

Discount Amount = Rs 15,625 - Rs 12,500 = Rs 3,125

Answer: The discount offered is Rs 3,125.

iii. What is the amount of VAT? (भ्याट रकम कति हो?)

भ्याट रकम = Selling Price with VAT - Selling Price without VAT

VAT Amount = Rs 14,125 - Rs 12,500 = Rs 1,625

Answer: The amount of VAT is Rs 1,625.

Question 4:

Commission Calculation (कमीशनको गणना)

Mrs. Nanu is an employee in a readymade shop with monthly salary of Rs 15800. She is also given a commission of 1.2% on the monthly sales. If the monthly sales amount to Rs 10,80,000.

Solution:

i. What commission amount did she receive? (उहाँले कति कमीशन पाउनुभयो?)

कमीशन रकम = Commission Rate * Monthly Sales

Commission Amount = 1.2% of Rs 10,80,000

Commission Amount = (1.2 / 100) * 10,80,000 = Rs 12,960

Answer: Mrs. Nanu received a commission of Rs 12,960.

ii. How much will be her monthly income? (उहाँको मासिक आम्दानी कति हुन्छ?)

कुल मासिक आम्दानी = Monthly Salary + Commission Amount

Total Monthly Income = Rs 15,800 + Rs 12,960 = Rs 28,760

Answer: Her total monthly income will be Rs 28,760.

iii. How much should be the monthly sales so that she can receive Rs 32,000 as the total monthly income? (कुल मासिक आम्दानी ३२,००० पाउनको लागि मासिक बिक्री कति हुनुपर्छ?)

चाहिएको आम्दानी = Rs 32,000

चाहिएको कमीशन रकम = Rs 32,000 - Rs 15,800 = Rs 16,200

Let S be the required monthly sales. (मासिक बिक्री S मानौं।)

Commission Amount = 1.2% of S

16200 = 0.012 * S

S = 16200 / 0.012 = 13,50,000

Answer: The monthly sales should be Rs 13,50,000 to achieve a total monthly income of Rs 32,000.

Question 5:

Profit and Bonus Calculation (नाफा र बोनसको गणना)

A bakery factory distributed 10% of its profit among its employees in a year. If each employee received Rs 4180 as the bonus, how much was the profit of the factory? Also find the amount of profit of the factory when each employee may receives Rs 5120 as the bonus as the same bonus rate 10%.

Solution:

Let P be the total profit of the factory. (जम्मा नाफा P मानौं।)

Let N be the number of employees. (जम्मा कर्मचारी N मानौं।)

हामीलाई थाहा छ:

  • जम्मा बोनस रकम = 10% of Profit = 0.10 * P
  • जम्मा बोनस रकम = Bonus per employee * Number of employees = 4180 * N

त्यसैले: 0.10 * P = 4180 * N

P = (4180 * N) / 0.10 = 41800 * N

प्रश्नमा कर्मचारीको संख्या (N) दिइएको छैन। त्यसैले, नाफालाई कर्मचारीको संख्याको आधारमा निकाल्नुपर्छ।

Part 1: How much was the profit of the factory? (कारखानाको नाफा कति थियो?)

नाफा = Rs 41,800 * N

Answer: The profit of the factory is Rs 41,800 multiplied by the number of employees (N).

Part 2: Find the amount of profit...when each employee may receives Rs 5120 as the bonus. (प्रत्येक कर्मचारीले ५१२० बोनस पाउँदा कारखानाको नाफा कति हुन्छ?)

यस केसमा, नयाँ बोनस प्रति कर्मचारी = Rs 5,120

Let P' be the new profit. (नयाँ नाफा P' मानौं।)

जम्मा बोनस = 10% of P' = N * 5120

0.10 * P' = 5120 * N

P' = (5120 * N) / 0.10 = 51200 * N

Answer: The new profit of the factory is Rs 51,200 multiplied by the number of employees (N).

Note: कर्मचारीको संख्या (N) नदिएको हुनाले, नाफाको ठोस मूल्य निकाल्न सकिँदैन। नाफा कर्मचारीको संख्यामा निर्भर गर्छ।
