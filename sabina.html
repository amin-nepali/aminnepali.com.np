Question 1

Percentages & Venn Diagram

Math पास 85%, English पास 87%, दुवैमा पास 81%. 18 जना फेल।

Simple Solution

i) At least one subject (कम्तीमा एक):

P(M ∪ E) = P(M) + P(E) − P(M ∩ E)

= 85% + 87% − 81% = 91%

Answer: 91% students passed in at least one subject.

ii) Total students (फेल आधारित):

Fail % = 100% − 91% = 9%

9% ≡ 18 students ⇒ 0.09 × T = 18 ⇒ T = 18/0.09 = 200

Answer: Total students = 200.

iii) Venn parts (diagram लागि प्रतिशत):

Only Math = 85 − 81 = 4%
Only English = 87 − 81 = 6%
Both = 81%
Outside (Fail) = 9%
Diagram बनाउँदा: M र E दुई circle overlap हुन्छ। बीच (overlap) मा 81%, M-only मा 4%, E-only मा 6%, circle बाहिर 9%.
Question 2

Income Tax (आयकर) – Slab अनुसार

Monthly salary Rs 75,000, DA Rs 2,500, Festival Rs 40,000, Life Insurance Rs 48,000.

Tax rule given: first 6,00,000 मा 1% Social Security Tax; 6–8 लाख भागमा 10%; 8–11 लाख भागमा 20%.

Simple Solution

i) Annual income (वार्षिक):

Monthly total = 75,000 + 2,500 = 77,500

Yearly (12 months) = 77,500 × 12 = 9,30,000

Add festival = 9,30,000 + 40,000 = 9,70,000

Answer: Annual income = Rs 9,70,000.

ii) Taxable income (कर लाग्ने):

9,70,000 − 48,000 = 9,22,000

Answer: Taxable = Rs 9,22,000.

iii) Tax calculation (सजिलै):

0–6,00,000: 1% SST
Tax = 1% × 6,00,000 = 6,000
6,00,001–8,00,000: 10%
Tax = 10% × 2,00,000 = 20,000
8,00,001–9,22,000: 20%
Part = 9,22,000 − 8,00,000 = 1,22,000
Tax = 20% × 1,22,000 = 24,400

Total tax = 6,000 + 20,000 + 24,400 = 50,400

Answer: Total tax = Rs 50,400.

Question 3

VAT & Discount (भ्याट र छुट)

Paid amount (VAT सहित) = Rs 14,125, Discount = 20%, VAT = 13%.

Simple Solution

पहिले VAT हटाउँ (get SP without VAT):

SP_with_VAT = SP × 1.13 ⇒ 14,125 = SP × 1.13 ⇒ SP = 14,125 / 1.13 = 12,500

i) Marked Price (MP):

SP = MP × (1 − 0.20) = MP × 0.80

12,500 = MP × 0.80 ⇒ MP = 12,500 / 0.80 = 15,625

Answer: MP = Rs 15,625.

ii) Discount amount:

= MP − SP = 15,625 − 12,500 = 3,125

Answer: Discount = Rs 3,125.

iii) VAT amount:

= Paid − SP = 14,125 − 12,500 = 1,625

Answer: VAT = Rs 1,625.

Question 4

Commission (कमीशन)

Salary = Rs 15,800 per month, Commission rate = 1.2%, Sales = Rs 10,80,000.

Simple Solution

i) Commission amount:

1.2% × 10,80,000 = (1.2/100) × 10,80,000 = 12,960

Answer: Commission = Rs 12,960.

ii) Monthly income (Salary + Commission):

15,800 + 12,960 = 28,760

Answer: Total monthly income = Rs 28,760.

iii) Required sales for income Rs 32,000:

Needed commission = 32,000 − 15,800 = 16,200

Let sales = S. 1.2% of S = 0.012S = 16,200 ⇒ S = 16,200 / 0.012 = 13,50,000

Answer: Sales needed = Rs 13,50,000.

Question 5

Profit & Bonus (नाफा र बोनस)

Factory ले profit को 10% bonus रूपमा सबै कर्मचारीलाई बाँड्छ। प्रति कर्मचारी bonus पहिले Rs 4180, पछि Rs 5120।

Simple Solution

मानौं total employees = N, profit = P.

Total bonus = 10% of P = 0.10P र उही कुरा = Bonus per person × N.

Part 1 (Rs 4180 प्रति कर्मचारी):

0.10P = 4180 × N ⇒ P = (4180 × N) / 0.10 = 41,800 × N

Answer: Profit = Rs 41,800 × N. (N नदिएकाले exact रकम निकालिँदैन)

Part 2 (Rs 5120 प्रति कर्मचारी):

0.10P' = 5120 × N ⇒ P' = (5120 × N) / 0.10 = 51,200 × N

Answer: New profit = Rs 51,200 × N.

Note: कर्मचारी संख्या N उस्तै रह्यो भन्ने मान्यता हो। N दिएको भए numerical profit पनि तुरुन्त निस्कन्थ्यो।
