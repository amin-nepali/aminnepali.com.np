Question 1:

Percentages and Venn-Diagrams

In an examination, 85% of the students passed in mathematics, 87% in English and 81% in both. If 18 students failed in the examination, find the total number of students and the percentage of students passed in at least one subject. Also, represent the information in a Venn-diagram.

Solution:

Let M be the set of students who passed in Mathematics and E be the set of students who passed in English.

Given:

  • Percentage passed in Mathematics, P(M) = 85%
  • Percentage passed in English, P(E) = 87%
  • Percentage passed in both subjects, P(M ∩ E) = 81%

i. What percent of the students passed in at least one subject?

The percentage of students passed in at least one subject is given by the formula for the union of two sets:

P(M ∪ E) = P(M) + P(E) - P(M ∩ E)

P(M ∪ E) = 85% + 87% - 81% = 91%

Answer: 91% of the students passed in at least one subject.

ii. If 18 students failed in the examination, find the total number of students.

The percentage of students who failed is the total percentage (100%) minus the percentage who passed in at least one subject.

Percentage Failed = 100% - P(M ∪ E) = 100% - 91% = 9%

We are given that 18 students failed. We can set up a proportion to find the total number of students (let's call it T).

9% of T = 18

0.09 * T = 18

T = 18 / 0.09 = 200

Answer: The total number of students is 200.

iii. Represent the above information in a Venn-diagram.

To create the Venn-diagram, we need to calculate the percentages of students who passed in only one subject and those who failed.

  • Only Mathematics: P(M only) = P(M) - P(M ∩ E) = 85% - 81% = 4%
  • Only English: P(E only) = P(E) - P(M ∩ E) = 87% - 81% = 6%
  • Both: P(M ∩ E) = 81%
  • Failed: 100% - 91% = 9%

Based on these calculations, the Venn-diagram would show two overlapping circles (M and E) within a rectangle (the universal set). The overlapping section would be labeled 81%, the non-overlapping part of circle M would be 4%, and the non-overlapping part of circle E would be 6%. The area outside the circles but inside the rectangle would be labeled 9%.

Question 2:

Income Tax Calculation

Mr. Shrestha married sales manager in a motorbike company having a monthly salary Rs 75000, a dearness allowance of Rs 2500 and Rs 40000 as a festival allowance. But he pays Rs 48000 as a premium of life insurance. It is given that 1% of social security tax is levied on the income up to Rs 6,00,000, 10% and 20% income tax on the incomes exceeding Rs 6,00,000 to Rs 8,00,000 and on the income exceeding 8,00,000 to Rs 11,00,000.

i. Find his annual income.

ii. What is the taxable income?

iii. Calculate the income tax paid by Mr. Shrestha.

Solution:

i. Find his annual income.

Annual Income = (Monthly Salary + Dearness Allowance) * 12 + Festival Allowance

Monthly Income = Rs 75,000 (Salary) + Rs 2,500 (Dearness Allowance) = Rs 77,500

Annual Income = (Rs 77,500 * 12) + Rs 40,000

Annual Income = Rs 9,30,000 + Rs 40,000 = Rs 9,70,000

Answer: Mr. Shrestha's annual income is Rs 9,70,000.

ii. What is the taxable income?

Taxable Income = Annual Income - Life Insurance Premium

Taxable Income = Rs 9,70,000 - Rs 48,000 = Rs 9,22,000

Answer: The taxable income is Rs 9,22,000.

iii. Calculate the income tax paid by Mr. Shrestha.

Income tax is calculated based on the taxable income of Rs 9,22,000 according to the given tax slabs.

  • Social Security Tax: 1% on income up to Rs 6,00,000.

    • Tax = 1% of Rs 6,00,000 = Rs 6,000

  • Tax on income from Rs 6,00,001 to Rs 8,00,000: 10% on the amount exceeding Rs 6,00,000.

    • Amount in this slab = Rs 8,00,000 - Rs 6,00,000 = Rs 2,00,000

    Tax = 10% of Rs 2,00,000 = Rs 20,000

  • Tax on income from Rs 8,00,001 to Rs 11,00,000: 20% on the amount exceeding Rs 8,00,000.

    • Amount in this slab = Taxable Income - Rs 8,00,000 = Rs 9,22,000 - Rs 8,00,000 = Rs 1,22,000

    Tax = 20% of Rs 1,22,000 = Rs 24,400

Total Income Tax = Tax (Slab 1) + Tax (Slab 2) + Tax (Slab 3)

Total Income Tax = Rs 6,000 + Rs 20,000 + Rs 24,400 = Rs 50,400

Answer: The total income tax paid by Mr. Shrestha is Rs 50,400.

Question 3:

VAT and Discount Calculation

A tourist paid Rs 14125 for a carved wooden window with a discount of 20% and 13% VAT.

i. Find the marked price of the window.

ii. Obtain the discount offered.

iii. What is the amount of VAT?

Solution:

Let MP be the Marked Price of the window.

Given:

  • Selling Price with VAT = Rs 14,125
  • Discount Rate = 20%
  • VAT Rate = 13%

Let SP be the Selling Price after the discount but before VAT.

Formula for Selling Price with VAT: SP with VAT = SP + (SP * VAT Rate) = SP * (1 + VAT Rate)

14125 = SP * (1 + 0.13)

14125 = SP * 1.13

SP = 14125 / 1.13 = 12500

The selling price after the discount is Rs 12,500.

i. Find the marked price of the window.

Formula for Selling Price with discount: SP = MP - (MP * Discount Rate) = MP * (1 - Discount Rate)

12500 = MP * (1 - 0.20)

12500 = MP * 0.80

MP = 12500 / 0.80 = 15625

Answer: The marked price of the window is Rs 15,625.

ii. Obtain the discount offered.

Discount Amount = Marked Price - Selling Price

Discount Amount = Rs 15,625 - Rs 12,500 = Rs 3,125

Answer: The discount offered is Rs 3,125.

iii. What is the amount of VAT?

VAT Amount = Selling Price with VAT - Selling Price without VAT

VAT Amount = Rs 14,125 - Rs 12,500 = Rs 1,625

Alternatively, VAT Amount = Selling Price * VAT Rate

VAT Amount = Rs 12,500 * 0.13 = Rs 1,625

Answer: The amount of VAT is Rs 1,625.

Question 4:

Commission Calculation

Mrs. Nanu is an employee in a readymade shop with monthly salary of Rs 15800. She is also given a commission of 1.2% on the monthly sales. If the monthly sales amount to Rs 10,80,000.

i. What commission amount did she receive?

ii. How much will be her monthly income?

iii. How much should be the monthly sales so that she can receive Rs 32,000 as the total monthly income?

Solution:

i. What commission amount did she receive?

Commission Amount = Commission Rate * Monthly Sales

Commission Amount = 1.2% of Rs 10,80,000

Commission Amount = (1.2 / 100) * 10,80,000 = 0.012 * 10,80,000 = Rs 12,960

Answer: Mrs. Nanu received a commission of Rs 12,960.

ii. How much will be her monthly income?

Total Monthly Income = Monthly Salary + Commission Amount

Total Monthly Income = Rs 15,800 + Rs 12,960 = Rs 28,760

Answer: Her total monthly income will be Rs 28,760.

iii. How much should be the monthly sales so that she can receive Rs 32,000 as the total monthly income?

Let S be the required monthly sales.

Desired Total Monthly Income = Rs 32,000

Commission Amount needed = Desired Total Income - Monthly Salary

Commission Amount needed = Rs 32,000 - Rs 15,800 = Rs 16,200

We know that Commission Amount = 1.2% of Sales (S)

16200 = 0.012 * S

S = 16200 / 0.012 = 13,50,000

Answer: The monthly sales should be Rs 13,50,000 to achieve a total monthly income of Rs 32,000.
