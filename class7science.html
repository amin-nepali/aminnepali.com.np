2. Very short answer questions

  1. a. What is relative density?
    The ratio of the density of a substance to the density of water at 4°C. Definition
  2. b. What is an element?
    A pure substance made of only one kind of atom; cannot be broken into simpler substances by chemical means. Definition
  3. c. What is metal?
    A substance typically hard, shiny, malleable, ductile, and a good conductor of heat & electricity. Definition
  4. d. Mention any one coinage metal.
    Gold (also: silver, copper). Gold

3. Short answer questions

  1. a. Name any two physical properties of metals.
    • Good conductor of heat and electricity.
    • Malleable (can be hammered into sheets) / Ductile (can be drawn into wires). Two properties
  2. b. If the relative density of gold is 19, find its density in the SI unit.
    Given: relative density = 19
    Density of water (4°C) = 1000 kg/m³

    Density of gold = relative density × density of water
    Density of gold = 19 × 1000 = 19000 kg/m³
