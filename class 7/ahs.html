🏃‍♀️ Involvement in Interactive Activities

Sexual desires naturally come into adolescents' minds because they have a lot of free time. The solution is to keep them occupied with engaging activities.

Recommended Activities:

  • Hiking & Camping: Outdoor adventures that build character
  • Sporting Events: Team activities that teach cooperation
  • Musical & Dance Events: Creative expression and skill development
  • Social Group Activities: Learning about responsibilities within groups

💡 Why Interactive Activities Work

These activities should be social so that adolescents can learn about their responsibilities within their group and develop healthy relationships with peers.

⚠️ Important Note

The goal is not to gather adolescents in a hall and lecture them, but to provide engaging, enjoyable activities that naturally keep them occupied and learning.

🧠 Counselling for Safe Sex Behavior

Adolescents often turn to the internet to understand their changing bodies and desires. However, this can lead to unhealthy habits that affect their mental development.

Understanding Brain Development

Your brain is still developing during adolescence. Certain behaviors can have long-term effects on your ability to concentrate and form healthy relationships.

⚠️ Mental Health Impact

While there may be no immediate physical problems, the mental aspects can be tremendous. Unhealthy habits can affect concentration, studying ability, and future relationship capacity.

Why Counselling is Important:

  • Helps you understand the long-term effects of your choices
  • Provides proper guidance during this developmental stage
  • Supports healthy brain development and concentration
  • Prepares you for healthy future relationships

📖 Sexuality Education

Most adolescents have little to no understanding of the changes they undergo during adolescence. Without proper sex education in schools, many turn to the internet for information.

🚨 The Problem with Internet Information

The internet is not a reliable source. There are many people telling youth different things, making it confusing. Bad advice can lead to bad consequences.

What Proper Sexuality Education Provides:

  • Accurate Information: About physical and emotional changes
  • Health Protection: Understanding how to protect your well-being
  • Decision Making: Skills to make informed choices about your health
  • Reliable Sources: Learning where to find trustworthy information

The Goal

Sexuality education aims to enable young people to protect their health and well-being, and make informed decisions about their sexual and reproductive health as they experience these changes.

🏥 Adolescent Friendly Health Services

Most adolescents lack proper information about sexual and reproductive health. The knowledge they have often comes from unreliable sources like friends or the internet.

⚠️ Why These Services Are Needed

Lack of proper information combined with hormonal changes can lead to unhealthy activities like unprotected sex, STDs, and unwanted pregnancy - potentially ruining young lives.
