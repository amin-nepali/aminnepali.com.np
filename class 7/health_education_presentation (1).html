Menstrual Health & Hygiene

🌸

Natural Process

Normal body function

🤗

Support Needed

Love and understanding

🧼

Clean Hygiene

Regular pad changes

Key Message: Menstruation is natural and normal

  • Support System: Girls need love and care, not isolation
  • Health Concerns: May feel weak, risk of anemia
  • Nutrition: Provide healthy foods and moral support
  • Hygiene Priority: Clean pads/clothes changed regularly
  • No Restrictions: Can participate in daily activities safely
Important: Avoid discrimination. Many restrictions are cultural myths, not medical necessities.

Sexual Abuse Prevention

Real-Life Example: Anita's Story

A gymnastics student experienced inappropriate touching from a substitute teacher. She felt uncomfortable but initially didn't know how to react.

What Students Should Know:

  • Trust your instincts - if something feels wrong, it probably is
  • Speak up immediately to trusted adults
  • It's never your fault
  • Help is available

How to Report & Get Help

🚨 Reporting Process

1
🏫 At School: Tell principal or trusted teacher immediately
2
🏠 Outside School: Tell parents or family members first
3
⛪ Religious Settings: Report to parents if religious leaders act inappropriately
Remember: Sexual abuse can happen to both boys and girls. Always report it ASAP (As Soon As Possible).

Discussion Questions:

  • Why is it important to speak up?
  • Who are the trusted adults in your life?
  • What should you do if someone asks you to keep inappropriate contact secret?

Understanding Puberty

Normal Physical Changes During Adolescence:

For Girls:

  • Breast development
  • Menstrual periods begin
  • Hip widening
  • Body hair growth

For Boys:

  • Voice changes
  • Muscle development
  • Facial and body hair
  • Growth spurts
Important: These changes are completely normal. Students need proper guidance and support during this time.

Importance of Proper Education

🧠 Healthy vs Unhealthy Information Sources

✅

Good Sources

👨‍🏫 Teachers
👨‍⚕️ Healthcare providers
📚 Educational materials

❌

Risky Sources

🌐 Random websites
📱 Social media
🤐 Unreliable peers

Why Students Need Guidance:

  • Natural Curiosity: Adolescents naturally have questions about their changing bodies
  • Reliable Information: School and family should provide accurate information
  • Internet Risks: Unreliable online sources can be harmful and misleading
  • Healthy Activities: Encourage sports, arts, social activities
Key Point: Keep students engaged in positive activities like camping, sports, music, and dance events to channel their energy constructively.
7/8

Mental Health & Well-being

Supporting Student Mental Health:

  • Open Communication: Create safe spaces for students to ask questions
  • Counseling Support: Professional guidance when needed
  • Mutual Respect: Teach students about consent and boundaries
  • Healthy Relationships: Education about respect in all relationships

Self-Control & Understanding:

Teaching students about patience, respect for others' feelings, and healthy ways to express emotions is crucial for their development.
