Section B: Short Answers

  1. Safety means staying away from danger. Safety education teaches us how to avoid accidents.
  2. Germs, dirty food, dirty water, and polluted air are main causes of diseases.
  3. Wear a mask and keep distance from sick people to prevent air borne diseases.
  4. Using dirty syringes or infected blood can spread dangerous diseases.
  5. Always use clean, new syringes and never share them with anyone.
  6. Sores and itching in private parts are symptoms of STDs.
  7. Cotton, bandage, antiseptic, scissors, and ointment are in a first aid box.
  8. Touching electric wires or wet switches can cause electric shock.
  9. Speeding and careless driving are main causes of road accidents.
  10. First aid saves lives and prevents injuries from becoming more serious.
  11. Wear shoes, avoid tall grass, use stick, and keep surroundings clean.

Section C: Long Answers

  1. Air borne diseases like tuberculosis, influenza, and pneumonia spread when we breathe germs from cough or sneeze. Prevent by wearing masks and avoiding sick people.
  2. Syringes can spread HIV and hepatitis if reused. Use new, sealed syringes and never share them.
  3. Electric shock first aid: Switch off power, use dry stick to move person, check breathing, and take to hospital.
  4. Sprain first aid: Rest, apply ice, wrap with bandage, and keep it raised above heart level.
  5. Snake bite: Don’t give food or water. Keep still, tie above bite, and take to hospital quickly.
