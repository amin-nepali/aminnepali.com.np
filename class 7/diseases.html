How do we get diseases?
We get diseases when germs enter our body through dirty water, dirty food, polluted air, or by touching or being close to sick people. Sometimes, mosquitoes or other insects can also bring germs. If we don’t keep clean, these germs can make us sick.
Breathing polluted air full of smoke and dust causes diseases. True or False?
True. Polluted air can harm our lungs and make us ill.
Name two examples of air borne diseases.
Tuberculosis and Influenza are examples of diseases spread through the air.
Which bacteria causes whooping cough?
It is caused by bacteria called Bordetella pertussis.
How can we prevent STDs?
We can prevent STDs by avoiding unsafe sexual contact and being careful about blood transfusions or needles.
What is the first sign of tonsillitis?
The first sign is usually a sore throat.
What is pharyngitis?
It is an illness like tonsillitis where the throat gets red and painful.
What is first aid?
It is the quick help we give to an injured or sick person before the doctor comes.
What things are in a first aid box?
It has medicines, bandages, antiseptics, scissors, and cotton.
If someone falls and cannot walk, what might have happened?
They may have fractured their leg or toes.
What should you do before helping someone who got electric shock?
Switch off the electricity first.
What signs show a person is in shock?
They might be shivering, weak, or unconscious.
Why do road accidents happen?
They happen because of rash and careless driving.
What does safety mean and why is safety education important?
Safety means staying away from danger. Safety education teaches us how to avoid accidents and keep ourselves safe.
How can we prevent air borne diseases?
We can wear masks and keep a safe distance from sick people.
How do dirty syringes spread diseases?
Using dirty syringes can spread HIV, hepatitis, and other dangerous diseases.
Name symptoms of STDs.
Sores and itching in private parts are symptoms of STDs.
What causes electric shock?
Touching electric wires or wet switches can cause electric shock.
What are the main causes of road accidents?
Speeding and careless driving.
Why is first aid important?
It saves lives and stops injuries from becoming worse.
How to avoid snake bite?
Wear shoes, avoid tall grass, use a stick while walking, and keep the surroundings clean.
Explain air borne diseases and how to prevent them.
Air borne diseases like tuberculosis, influenza, and pneumonia spread when germs from cough or sneeze go into the air and we breathe them. Wearing masks and avoiding sick people helps prevent them.
How can syringes spread diseases and how to prevent?
If syringes are used more than once, they can carry germs like HIV and hepatitis from one person to another. Always use a new, sealed syringe.
First aid for electric shock.
Switch off the power, move the person away with a dry stick, check if they are breathing, and take them to hospital quickly.
First aid for sprain.
Rest the injured part, put ice, wrap with a bandage, and keep it raised above heart level.
First aid for snake bite.
Do not give food or water, keep the person still, tie above the bite, and take them to hospital fast.
